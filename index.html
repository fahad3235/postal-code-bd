About Bangladesh postcodes

  • Bangladesh uses a 4-digit postcode system, introduced on 22 December 1986.
  • There are five General Post Offices: Dhaka GPO (1000), Metropolitan Dhaka (1100), Chattogram GPO (4000), Rajshahi GPO (6000) and Khulna GPO (9000).
  • This app lists district head offices plus Dhaka city area post offices — 92 entries. It is a reference subset, not the full national list of post offices.
